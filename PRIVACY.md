# Privacy — THOR Enterprise

**THOR Enterprise collects nothing. There is no telemetry, no analytics, no
phone-home, and no account, in this version.**

- Your code, your feature specs, and your ledger live on **your machine**, in your
  Postgres, in your git. Nothing is transmitted to the maintainer or any third party
  by THOR itself.
- The only network traffic THOR creates is the traffic **you configure**: calls to
  whatever model endpoint you choose (local Ollama means even that stays on your
  hardware), your own git host, and governed room egress under your allowlist.
- If a future version ever adds any optional, clearly-disclosed telemetry, it will be
  off by default and this document will change first.

You can use THOR freely, for good purpose — to build something that helps humanity.
